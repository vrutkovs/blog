# Tech Industry & Culture

* [Why Old Software Sometimes Feels Better](https://deadparrotbbs.com/why-old-software-sometimes-feels-better/)

  Old software often feels better because it was smaller, more direct, and less interested in managing the user. Stable interfaces, local functionality, and user ownership made tools feel reliable; subscriptions, constant change, and complexity make modern software feel like service territory.

  Tags: Software, Tool, Control, Speed, Ownership

* [1Password wades into a right-wing mess after funding a Linux project](https://www.theverge.com/tech/988536/1password-dhh-linux-controversy)

  1Password faces backlash after pledging $300,000 to the foundation behind Omarchy. The company's CEO says funding popular software does not endorse its creator's racist, anti-vax, and anti-immigrant views, but customers and employees see that separation as inadequate.

  Tags: 1Password, Linux, Controversy, Funding, Ethics

* [Neil Fraser: News: .name Termination](https://neil.fraser.name/news/2026/09/03/)

  Verisign and ICANN plan to eliminate the third level of the `.name` domain hierarchy in February 2027. That will take Fraser's website, email, and connected IoT devices offline, while creating a risk that his second-level domain could later be registered by someone else.

  Tags: Domain, Termination, ICANN, Verisign, Website

* [If Eastern Europeans reported the world like the world reports Eastern Europe](https://www.badbaltic.com/if-eastern-europeans-reported-the-world-like-the-world-reports-eastern-europe/)

  This satirical guide reverses familiar reporting tropes about Eastern Europe and applies them to Canada, Alaska, Belgium, and Ireland. It exposes how defining countries through former occupiers, denying agency, flattening language groups, and reporting from imperial capitals turns bias into routine journalism.

  Tags: Satire, Journalism, Bias, Imperialism, Stereotype

* [Write for Yourself First](https://nikhil.bearblog.dev/write-for-yourself-first/)

  After publishing daily for a year, writing becomes less about trends, algorithms, or audience growth and more about saying something honestly. Regular practice, authentic expression, and writing as if speaking to a friend offer better guidance than chasing attention.

  Tags: Writing, Authenticity, Consistency, Self, Practice
