# Artificial Intelligence & Society

* [The AI Pascal’s Wager](https://ploum.net/2026-10-01-pascal_wager.html)

  Open-source projects should state whether they accept AI-generated contributions. A middle position defaults to acceptance, while rejecting them protects project independence and code quality until the technology improves enough to justify revisiting the decision.

  Tags: AI, OpenSource, Policy, Contribution, Wager

* [There's little that's "inevitable" about AI](https://deadsimpletech.com/blog/llms-arent-inevitable)

  The glib response here would be to say that no technology is ever inevitable and that everything is contingent. This has the advantages of being technically true and sounding wise, but unfortunately technologies very much exist that a reasonable person might well label that way. Firearms are a good example of the latter case: once they've been developed, you quite quickly hit the situation where if you want to maintain your independence and safety in a society, you have to either use one or have one used on your behalf: no other options really work.

  Tags: AI, Technology, Inevitability, LLM, Society
