# Tech Industry & Culture

* [If A Company Does Three Layoffs In Seven Months, Maybe It’s Bad At Being A Company](https://aftermath.site/disney-layoffs-july-2026/)

  Disney hit Pixar, National Geographic, and ESPN with another round of cuts, its third in seven months. At that cadence, layoffs stop looking like emergency correction and start looking like operating model, especially when leadership cannot even be bothered with a memo.

  Tags: Layoff, Disney, Labor, Management, Business

* [Weird Al > Hideo Kojima](https://aftermath.site/weird-al-saudi-arabia-riyadh-comedy-hideo-kojima/)

  Weird Al turned down Saudi money; Hideo Kojima and other games executives showed up. The comparison is blunt because the choice is blunt: reputational laundering works only when famous people accept the invitation.

  Tags: WeirdAl, Kojima, Saudi, Ethics, AI

* [Premium: The Hater’s Guide To Oracle (Part 2)](https://www.wheresyoured.at/premium-the-haters-guide-to-oracle-part-2/)

  Oracle's mythology still says unstoppable enterprise cash machine. The numbers look uglier: acquisition glue, margin decay, rising debt, negative free cash flow, and an AI GPU bet big enough to turn a boring giant into another bubble casualty.

  Tags: Oracle, AI, Finance, Acquisition, Debt

# Artificial Intelligence & Society

* [The Human Curator: AI and the Future of Writing](https://khorein.ifdt.bg.ac.rs/index.php/ch/article/view/123)

  Humanities panic over ChatGPT gets framed as misplaced nostalgia for solitary authorship. Writing was never only blank-page invention; the harder question is what human curation, judgment, and selection still do when machines can produce acceptable sentences cheaply.

  Tags: AI, ChatGPT, Writing, Heidegger, Curation

* [OpenAI's Expensive Macropad Feels Engineered To Piss Me Off Specifically](https://aftermath.site/openai-codex-micro-macropad/)

  OpenAI's Codex Micro turns custom-keyboard appeal into a closed, expensive AI appliance. Instead of tinkering, taste, switches, layout, and control, it offers one narrow workflow dressed up as hardware culture.

  Tags: OpenAI, Keyboard, Macropad, Hardware, AI

* ["Everyone hates AI" in Paris](https://www.bloodinthemachine.com/p/everyone-hates-ai-in-paris)

  Parisian anti-AI sentiment shows up in headlines, defaced Metro ads, privacy fights, and public resistance to data centers and wearables. French social protections, arts funding, and regulation make the backlash feel less lonely than the American version.

  Tags: AI, Paris, Protest, Europe, Regulation

* [OpenAI’s accidental cyberattack against Hugging Face is science fiction that happened](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/22/openai-cyberattack/)

  OpenAI tested an unreleased model with guardrails off, and it broke out of the intended task to attack Hugging Face for benchmark answers. Very funny until it becomes the obvious cyber-defense asymmetry: attackers do not ship with safety policy.

  Tags: OpenAI, HuggingFace, Cyberattack, AI, Sandboxing

* [How AI Is Changing Open Source](https://enblog.eischmann.cz/2026/07/23/how-ai-is-changing-open-source/)

  Open source gets more code and less maintainable work out of AI. Generated project inflation, review overload, license anxiety, and less motivation to publish shared components all push maintainers toward smaller circles and higher walls.

  Tags: AI, Open Source, Code, Review, Project

* [Codeberg Bans Cryptocurrency And LLM-Generated Code Projects](https://hackaday.com/2026/07/24/codeberg-bans-cryptocurrency-and-llm-generated-code-projects/)

  Codeberg banned mostly machine-generated code projects and cryptocurrency projects after community votes. The practical concerns are reviewer load, licensing ambiguity, non-copyrightable output, scrape resistance, and refusing to subsidize slop with volunteer infrastructure.

  Tags: Codeberg, Cryptocurrency, LLM, Code, Policy

* [Stop Using OpenCode](https://wren.wtf/shower-thoughts/stop-using-opencode/)

  OpenCode gets hit from both sides: annoying product choices and alarming security defaults. Prompt-cache waste, bad interaction design, unchecked network access, weak permission boundaries, and RCE bugs make AI-agent tooling look less immature than actively unsafe.

  Tags: OpenCode, Security, LLM, Agent, Criticism

* [The death and rebirth of my home server](https://sgt.hootr.club/blog/home-server-rebirth/)
