# Artificial Intelligence & Society

* [Embla 1: A reasoning model built for phones](https://sevren.ai/blog/embla-1)

  Embla 1 is an open-weights reasoning model designed to run offline on phones. At 4-bit precision, it averages 57.4 across five benchmarks and responds in 1.77 seconds on an iPhone 18 Pro.

  Tags: Embla, Reasoning, Mobile, Benchmark, Quantization

* [‘AI’ might be the end of our careers](https://www.baldurbjarnason.com/2026/08-ai-might-be-the-end-of-our-careers/)

  AI coding tools could reduce demand for specialized developers, increase the supply of available workers, and weaken wages and bargaining power. The piece calls for resistance to the routine use of AI-generated code.

  Tags: Automation, Career, Software, Labor, AI

# Software Engineering & Cloud

* [Git Beyond the Basics: Worktrees, Cherry-Picks and Signed Commits](https://blog.hofstede.it/git-beyond-the-basics-worktrees-cherry-picks-and-signed-commits/)

  Git worktrees make it easier to work on parallel branches, while interactive rebases, cherry-picks, and reflogs help clean up history and recover mistakes. The guide also covers signing commits with SSH or OpenPGP keys.

  Tags: Git, Worktree, Rebase, Security, Workflow

* [What Maintaining SwiftTerm Taught Me About AI Contributions - Miguel de Icaza](https://tirania.org/blog/archive/2026/Nov-20.html)

  Maintaining SwiftTerm has brought Miguel de Icaza both low-quality AI-generated pull requests and useful patches. He favors human accountability and careful review over blanket bans based on how code was written.

  Tags: Opensource, Ai, Maintainer, Review, Github

* [“…and the proof is checked in Lean”](https://xenaproject.wordpress.com/2026/11/12/and-the-proof-is-checked-in-lean/)

  A Lean proof needs more than a successful check before its mathematical claim can be trusted. Check that the formal statement matches the intended theorem, the code compiles, the proof adds no unauthorized axioms, and the kernel is sound.

  Tags: Lean, Mathematics, Verification, Proof, Ai

* [Welcome to Software Beirut](https://archive.is/QsTox)

  AI-assisted vulnerability discovery could expose flaws faster than teams can patch them. Cuts to US cybersecurity agencies such as CISA could leave public services and smaller organizations with less support when attacks follow.

  Tags: Cybersecurity, Software, Infrastructure, Vulnerability, Technology
