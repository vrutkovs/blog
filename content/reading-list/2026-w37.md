# Tech Industry & Culture

* [A/I Shuts Down Stay Human · Keep the Internet free](https://keepitfree.ai/announcements/a/i-shuts-down-stay-human/)

  Autistici/Inventati shuts down after 25 years, unable to protect users once designated a global terrorist organization. The collective urges continuing resistance offline and promises data backup instructions before services end.

  Tags: Shutdown, Privacy, Resistance, Security, Human

* [The other WTC Attack](https://blog.jimmac.eu/posts/the-other-wtc-attack/)

  Jakub posts about his 1993 visit to the World Trade Center, unaware of the truck bombing that had struck months earlier. The post covers the attack's structural damage, casualties, and the photoluminescent stair markings it inspired, later credited with aiding 9/11 evacuations.

  Tags: WTC, Bomb, Attack, History, Safety

* [The Dark Forest Theory of the Internet | by Yancey Strickler](https://archive.is/mUwQM)

  Liu Cixin's Dark Forest Theory applied to the internet: users retreat from ad-filled, tracked mainstream platforms into private newsletters, podcasts, and invite-only groups for psychological safety. The retreat aids individual wellness but risks ceding public forums to negative influences.

  Tags: Internet, Privacy, Digital, Social, Retreat

# Artificial Intelligence & Society

* [The real AI jobs apocalypse](https://www.bloodinthemachine.com/p/the-real-ai-jobs-apocalypse)

  A conversation with sociologist Dr. Alex Hanna on AI's real employment impact, worker discontent, and the human labor behind 'autonomous' tech like Waymo. NYC bans AI in K-8 schools, California pushes broad AI legislation, and a 'tech-forward future' looks more like a mirage than a promise.

  Tags: AI, Labor, Policy, Ethics, Resistance

* [Top mathematicians are outraged by OpenAI’s methods](https://archive.is/IV6u2)

  Twenty-four Fields Medal winners sign an open letter over OpenAI's claim to have solved Navier-Stokes with a proof lacking traditional explanation. Solving problems without conceptual understanding strips discovery of its ancillary benefits and risks "cognitive offloading" that hollows out abstract mathematical work.

  Tags: AI, Math, OpenAI, Outrage, Technology

* [Everyone should slow down AI development except for me](https://xeiaso.net/notes/2026/everyone-slowdown-but-me/)

  A call for a global pause in frontier AI, undercut immediately by its own motive: buying time for Techaro's Lygma AGI lab to catch up and dominate with 'Intelliga' models — ideally on the way to giving people cat ears. The financial and market motive is stated outright.

  Tags: AI, AGI, Pause, Catgirl, Business
