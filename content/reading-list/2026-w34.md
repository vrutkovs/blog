# Tech Industry & Culture

* [Dogshit](http://hughhowey.com/dogshit/)

  In the late 80s, a Microsoft manager coined “eat their own dogfood” to push internal use of LAN Manager. The practice made sense when teams needed to trust tools before asking others to trust them, but applying it to AI exposes a deeper problem: generated output can feed model training and cause quality to collapse.

Tags: AI, Watermark, Dogfood, Model, Content

# Artificial Intelligence & Society

* [I would never accuse anyone of using AI](http://tofutush.github.io/posts/2026-08-18.html)

  Public accusations of AI use can harm non-native speakers, neurodivergent people, and artists forced to prove their work is authentic. AI and human writing can be indistinguishable, so open disclosure and room for art to adapt offer better paths than suspicion.

Tags: AI, Art, Accusation, Disclosure, Perception

* [Memory that arrives on time](http://xn--grn-sna.name/posts/2026-08-17-memory-that-arrives-on-time/)
